Rumee — Flipkart Tab

Sales, ads, and search visibility — one platform

LIVE
2026-07-24

The Flipkart tab answers three separate questions: how are sales and returns trending, which ad campaigns are actually profitable, and which search keywords are driving organic (unpaid) product views. Each question is fed by a different Flipkart report, landing in a different Firestore collection.

the shared pipeline / the tab itself
01

Revenue and order-count come from two different reports

Flipkart's Views/Traffic report includes a "sales" figure, but it's really a byproduct of the traffic report -- not a trustworthy daily order count. Because of that, a separate fk_orders_daily collection exists, fed by Flipkart's real Fulfilment Orders report, purely so the trend chart's order counts are accurate. GMV still has no better daily source than the traffic report, so that part stays as-is.

02

Sales & returns trend

The KPI cards and "GMV vs Net Settlement" / "Orders vs Customer Returns vs Courier RTO" charts show how much of gross sales actually gets paid out, and how much of what you sell comes back. A return-reasons breakdown sits alongside, so a spike in returns has an obvious next question already answered.

03

Ad Campaigns -- which spend is actually working

Every campaign's clicks/spend/revenue roll up into ROAS (revenue ÷ spend) and CTR (clicks ÷ views), sortable, so it's obvious at a glance which campaigns are profitable and which are quietly burning budget.

04

Catalogue Keywords -- organic visibility, not paid

This is a completely separate signal from ad keywords: which search terms are driving unpaid product views on Flipkart's own search, per SKU. Filterable per SKU, so you can check "what are people actually searching to find this listing" without any ad spend involved.

05

Claims

Total claims, how many are still open, and how much approved-but-not-yet-credited value is sitting in Flipkart's claims process -- the "how much money is stuck" number.

Worth knowing

If the daily order-count trend ever looks off, remember GMV/revenue and order-count are sourced from two genuinely different Flipkart reports on two different schedules -- a gap in one doesn't necessarily mean a gap in the other.
