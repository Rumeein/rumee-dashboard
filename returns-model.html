Rumee — Returns Tab

One scan, three downstream effects

LIVE
2026-07-24

The Returns Scanner writes to a Google Sheet, not Firestore -- it's fully self-contained. But the one signal it captures per scan (is the earring, box, and chain intact or damaged) quietly feeds two completely different downstream systems: manual QC bonus payouts, and the live pipeline's stock credit-back.

what you do on the tab the two things that same scan quietly feeds
01

Scan or type -- both paths converge

A rear-camera scan or a manual/keyboard-wedge entry both land in the same parser. Order-ID prefixes route by platform first (Flipkart, Amazon, Meesho patterns), everything else is matched against a courier-AWB pattern table (Ekart, Shadowfax, Delhivery, Valmo, XpressBees, Amazon's own ATSPL codes).

02

The ordered-SKU lookup drives the Bahubali default

The pipeline publishes ONE Firestore doc, fully overwritten every run, resolving Order ID (and best-effort AWB) to the real ordered SKU -- confirmed via that platform's own Returns report where available, otherwise estimated from a 45-day Orders window. This is what auto-fills "Ordered: {sku}" on the review card, and what decides whether the Chain-Intact toggle defaults to Intact (Bahubali only) or stays blank (OG/Base) -- resolved from Product Master's real variation type, never guessed from the SKU text.

03

Three independent Intact/Damaged toggles

Earring and Box default to Intact automatically when the card opens; Chain only defaults when the resolved SKU is genuinely Bahubali. Saving appends one frozen-column-order row to the Sheet and immediately refocuses the input for the next scan -- zero clicks between one return and the next.

04

One signal, two completely separate consumers

The same intact/damaged data captured here is read by (a) a manual QC-bonus calculation Jaiswal does outside this app, and (b) the live pipeline's stock credit-back -- an intact return credits stock back per component, matched to whichever BOM ingredient type applies (earring ↔ base_earring, box ↔ packaging, chain ↔ intermediate). Neither of these duplicates the Sheet; both just read it.

Worth knowing

Mapping a previously-unresolved order SKU (via the Stock tab's Needs Mapping card) only fixes resolution going forward -- past unresolved sales already skipped their stock movement and are never silently backfilled by that mapping.

Rough edge

Two Ekart routing barcode shapes leave the AWB blank and auto-trigger a second camera scan 300ms later -- on a desktop with no rear camera, this means closing the camera prompt and typing/scanning the AWB into the manual field instead.
